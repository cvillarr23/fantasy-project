Fantasy Project is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by ESPN or The Walt Disney Company. "ESPN" is used only to say which service the project works with.

- **Unofficial endpoints.** The project works with ESPN Fantasy through undocumented endpoints and browser automation, not a public API. ESPN can change them at any time, so features can break without notice.
- **Personal use only.** Use your own ESPN account, on leagues you are a member of.
- **No scraping.** The project doesn't scrape ESPN. It isn't meant for collecting data about other leagues or other users, or for using ESPN at scale.
- **Terms of service.** Automating actions such as lineup changes, waiver claims, trades and draft picks may conflict with ESPN's terms of use. You are responsible for complying with them, and you use the project at your own risk.
- **Rate and impact.** The bots act on one league at the volume of a person managing their team: periodic syncs of that league's data and individual roster moves.
