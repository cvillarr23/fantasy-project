# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Review the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as real names, emails, league or team IDs, or credentials.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews in every fantasy-* repository, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/cvillarr23/fantasy-project/security/advisories/new) addressed to the maintainer if the report is sensitive. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, depending on the severity and pattern.
